**Copying a reference's own graphics** (e.g. an aesthetic bow): **lift it from the reference** —
cut the sticker out of the reference video (it stays still while the video moves underneath),
save it as a transparent PNG in a "learned from references" library, and place it in edits the
way the reference does. Every element stays editable in the timeline editor.
